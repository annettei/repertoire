      About Repertoire

      Repertoire is for pianists who want a say in what they play next. Name the piece you’re learning, choose a kind of piece, and hear five places to go from there. Keep track of what you’re playing in My Repertoire: your program holds the pieces you’re working on now, next to the ones you want to play and the ones you’ve learned.

      Each kind of piece has its own look, because a waltz and a nocturne shouldn’t feel the same: the opening painting and the page colors change with the form, and every recommendation shows a painting from its composer’s homeland, from their own era when the museum has one. Paintings come from the Art Institute of Chicago’s public-domain collection.

      Pieces are matched by genre and approximate difficulty. Levels are rough estimates and worth checking with a teacher. Audio previews come from public-domain recordings and Apple Music.
